cities.json: city names from GeoNames

Source: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip
        (populated places over 15,000 people, and capitals)
        https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities500.zip
        (only for the hand-listed famous small places in tools/famous-places.txt)
        https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/alternateNamesV2.zip
        (English alternate names and abbreviations, such as "New York" and "NYC")
        https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/admin1CodesASCII.txt and countryInfo.txt
        (state, province and country names)
Fetched: 2026-11-15
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Credit: GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org/

Changed from the source: only the name, ASCII name, up to five English alternate names,
up to two abbreviations, country code, population, whether it is well known (capital,
state capital, hand list, or population) and the state or province are kept; city sections (PPLX), numbered districts and abandoned or destroyed places are
left out; sorted by population, biggest first.
Rebuilt with games/hot-potato/lab/tools/make-cities.py in the n8-party-games repository.

countries.json: country names from GeoNames

Source: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/countryInfo.txt
        (the countries and territories of ISO 3166, their continents and populations)
        https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/alternateNamesV2.zip
        (English other names and abbreviations, such as "Holland", "Burma" and "UAE")
Fetched: 2026-11-15
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Credit: GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org/
Changed from the source: only the name, up to eight English other names, up to three
abbreviations of three or four letters and the continent are kept; formal names ("Republic
of ..."), names of former states and the names in tools/countries-drop.txt are left out;
Serbia and Montenegro and the Netherlands Antilles (no longer existing) are left out;
England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland are added by hand (tools/countries-extra.txt);
sorted by population, biggest first.
Rebuilt with games/hot-potato/lab/tools/make-categories.py in the n8-party-games repository.


animals.json and food.json: animals, foods and drinks from Open English WordNet

Source: Open English WordNet 2025 edition, https://en-word.net/
        https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet/releases/download/2025-edition/english-wordnet-2025.xml.gz
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Credit: Open English WordNet (the Global WordNet Association), based on Princeton WordNet 3.0
        (Copyright 2006 by Princeton University; WordNet licence, which allows use, copying,
        modification and distribution with this notice).
Which entries are well known (in the default list) is chosen with the word counts of
FrequencyWords en_50k (see words.json above, CC BY-SA 4.0), so animals.json and food.json
are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
Fetched: 2026-11-15
Changed from the source: only the names of nouns under WordNet's chordates, invertebrates,
young animals and larvae (animals; not humans) and under food, nutrient, drink and edible nut
(food and drink; not animal feed, diets, food stores or vitamins) are kept, with a broad
group (mammal, bird, fish ...; drink); scientific names and the names in
tools/animals-drop.txt and tools/food-drop.txt are left out; tools/animals-add.txt and
tools/food-add.txt mark well-known names the word counts miss.
Rebuilt with games/hot-potato/lab/tools/make-categories.py in the n8-party-games repository.
