cities.json: city names from GeoNames

Source: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip
        (populated places over 15,000 people, and capitals)
        and https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/alternateNamesV2.zip
        (English alternate names and abbreviations, such as "New York" and "NYC")
Fetched: 2026-11-15
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Credit: GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org/

Changed from the source: only the name, ASCII name, up to five English alternate names,
up to two abbreviations, country code and population are kept; city sections (PPLX) and
abandoned or destroyed places are left out; sorted by population, biggest first.
Rebuilt with lab/hot-potato/tools/make-cities.py in the n8-party-games repository.
