    How words are checked

    Only the letters A to Z count, after accents are removed: São Paulo starts with S and ends with O. Spaces and punctuation are skipped: New York ends with K. The chain goes on from the name you said, so "New York" ends with K, while "NYC" plays as New York City and ends with Y. A place, or a name, said once this game cannot be said again.

    What was heard is matched against about 32,000 places: every guess the phone returns is tried, exact names first, then names that sound alike (bigger cities first, and only among names starting with the right letter).

    Privacy

    The microphone is used only while a turn is listening. This page stores and sends nothing. The browser's speech recognition may send the audio to its maker's servers (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone) to turn it into text.
