Cork & Bell: UI pass

Round two (arcade polish, every menu and state) is on the page beside this one.

Before and after, at desktop (1440 x 900), a phone held sideways (844 x 390, and a smaller 667 x 375) and a phone held upright (390 x 844). Every still is from headless Chrome on the real graphics card, muted.

1. The start screen

"There needs to be a little bit more clear about enabling sound and playing the battle."

A painted board now spells out the three choices: play alone, battle with friends (make a room or join with a PIN), and the sound, which says whether it is on or off. The house keeps playing behind it. A click or a touch on a target still takes the gun at once.

2. The time and the score

"The time and the score on the bottom, I think that could be a little bit better."

The ticket now has two big figures, time and score, with the run and the corks left on a stub below the perforation. A run that is multiplying shows by how much. The last ten seconds paint the time red. During a round the sound switch at the top right says "Sound: off" or "Sound: on".

3. Ricochets

"I feel like ricochets should be even more points."

A ricochet hit (the cork has already bounced off something) now pays three times over instead of double, in solo play and in battles alike, and raises a paper flag over the points.
