Rooms

One party room, many skins. Every room keeps the layout exactly: the code and the door at the left, a lamp and a pool of light under each game, the Tonight board at the right. A skin changes the wall, the floor, the lamps, the lettering, the signs and a few small things.

Rooms marked live can be played in today, in the 3D look. Rooms marked concept are pictures of an idea: the same scene, posed, with four pieces in the middle of a vote.
